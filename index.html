Solar Water Heaters

Eliminate water heating expenses completely. Our systems supply hot water 365 days a year using state-of-the-art absorption technology.

Available Technology Types:

ETC Type (Evacuated Tube Collector)

Vacuum glass tubes. Works efficiently even in winter & cloudy days. Extremely cost-effective.

Heavy copper absorber. Sturdy metallic frame, ideal for pressurized systems and hard-water regions.

Capacities: 100 LPD, 200 LPD, 250 LPD, 300 LPD, 500 LPD & Industrial scale

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common queries about government subsidies, ETC vs FPC collector durability, maintenance schedules, and expected savings calculations.

Ask Custom Question

Typically, rooftop solar panels cut your electricity bills by 80% to 90%. Depending on system sizing, roof orientation, and sunlight, you will only be paying the basic service grid fee charges. ROI is usually achieved within 3.5 to 5 years.

ETC (Evacuated Tube Collector) uses double-walled vacuum glass tubes. It absorbs heat faster, performs well in cold/cloudy climates, and is cheaper. FPC (Flat Plate Collector) uses copper sheets with toughened glass cover. It is highly impact-resistant, lasts longer, and is recommended for high-pressure setups or hard-water areas.

Yes, individual residential houses, apartments, and group housing societies qualify for the national rooftop solar subsidy portal. As an empanelled agency, Balaji Solar handles your registration, technical documentation, inspection uploads, and final subsidy payouts directly.

Solar panels have no moving parts and only need basic cleaning. Washing the dust off panels every 10-15 days with water maintains solar efficiency. Water heater glass tubes also need routine exterior rinsing to prevent dirt scaling.
